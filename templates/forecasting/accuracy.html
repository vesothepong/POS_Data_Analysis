Forecast Accuracy

Each product's monthly history is split into training months and a test period (last ~20%). The model is trained on the training months only, predicts the test months, and is compared with the actual sales. The "average baseline" column shows the error you would get by simply predicting the training average, so a lower MAE than the baseline shows the regression is learning the trend.
